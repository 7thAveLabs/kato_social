Message sounds on the thread Reels: Epidemic Sound "User Interface, Alert, Notification, Message, Text 05" (sent) and "Text 02" (received).

How a track is applied: the video stream is kept as rendered; the track is cut from its first full section to the video's length,
faded in over 0.3 s and out over 1.4 s, levelled to about -17 LUFS, and mixed with any message sounds at the moments messages appear.
Do not reuse a track within 4 weeks.
